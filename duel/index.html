⚔ The Duel of Honor ⚔

Philadelphia, 1776

Two gentlemen of the Continental Congress settle their differences in the time-honored tradition.

Rules of Engagement

  • Wait for the green light! Shooting early means your shot goes wide.
  • First to fire after green wins - unless they shot early!
  • Reconsider - raise your pistol to spare your opponent. Only revealed after green.
  • If you reconsider but your opponent shoots you: they win but lose honor (down a level)
  • If both reconsider: both gain honor (up a level)
  • Win 3 duels in a row to gain honor (up a level)
